Satu Data Statistik Republik Indonesia

National statistics portal integrating verified macroeconomic, demographic, socio-economic, and trade accounts. Dedicated to public transparency, evidence-based policymaking, and open data access.

Official Data Custodians

  • Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) — Sensus & Berita Resmi Statistik (BRS)
  • Bank Indonesia (BI) — Kerangka Kebijakan Moneter & Neraca Pembayaran
  • Kementerian Keuangan RI — Laporan APBN & Realisasi Fiskal

Data Standards & Methodology

Data compiled under the UN Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics and IMF Special Data Dissemination Standard (SDDS). Zero third-party tracker or network calls.
